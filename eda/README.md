

SATACT Codebook:

** variables provided
State: The state the school is in 
District: School District 
School: School Name 
Combokey: Identification Key 
Justice Facility: Yes/No indicator of if the school is a justice facility
Hispanic Male: Number of Hispanic Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
Hispanic Female: Number of Hispanic Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
Native American Male: Number of Native American Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
Native American Female: Number of Native American Female Students taking the SAT/ACT
Asian Male: Number of Asian Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
Asian Female: Number of Asian Female Students taking the SAT/ACT
Pacific Islander Male: Number of Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
Pacific Islander Female : Number of Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander Female Students taking the SAT/ACT
Black Male: Number of Black Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
Black Female: Number of Black Female Students taking the SAT/ACT
White Male: Number of White Male Students taking the SAT/ACT 
White Female: Number of White Female Students taking the SAT/ACT 
Two or more races Male: Number of Male Students identifying as two or more races taking the SAT/ACT
Two or more races Female: Number of Female Students identifying as two or more races taking the SAT/ACT
Total Male: Total number of Male students taking the SAT/ACT
Total Female: Total number of Female students taking the SAT/ACT
EL Male: Number of EL Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
EL Female: Number of EL Female Students taking the SAT/ACT

** variable we added
IDEA Male: Number of IDEA Male Students taking the SAT/ACT
IDEA Female:  Number of EL Female Students taking the SAT/ACT 
Total: Total number of Students taking the SAT/ACT 
Total White: Total Number of White Students taking the SAT/ACT
Total Nonwhite: Total Number of Nonwhite Students taking the SAT/ACT 
Total EL: Total Number of EL Students taking the SAT/ACT
Total IDEA: Total Number of IDEA Students taking the SAT/ACT
